# Part 1 — Answer Key

1. **A** — The question asks for a location. Only A provides directions.
   - Statement: *Excuse me, do you know where the registrar's office is?*

2. **B** — The speaker expresses uncertainty about credit transfer — advising them to see an advisor is the most helpful response.
   - Statement: *I was thinking about switching my major from biology to chemistry, but I'm not sure if my credits would transfer.*

3. **A** — A acknowledges the issue and provides a resolution — this is the natural response from a professor.
   - Statement: *Professor, I noticed the reading assignment for next week isn't posted on the course website yet.*

4. **B** — The speaker is expressing frustration (not just stating a fact). B acknowledges the frustration and offers actionable advice.
   - Statement: *I've been waiting for the shuttle for twenty minutes. This is the third time this week it's been late.*
